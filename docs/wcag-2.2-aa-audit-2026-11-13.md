## Corrections to numbers I gave mid-audit

- I earlier reported "106 targets, 0 under 24×24" for 2.5.8. That set was the option rows and buttons; it excluded the native `<input>` boxes. The honest figure is **192 targets, 46 raw inputs at 23.2 px**, whose effective target is their 334×52 label — the verdict does not change, the reasoning does.
- I flagged `#cf3636` on `#eaf0f6` as a contrast failure (4.32:1). It is a failing **token pair**, but not a failing **rendering**: no error text is ever painted on that tint. `docs/wcag-2.2-aa-conformance.md`'s "4.53" is correct — it refers to the error surface `#fdf2f2`. My suspicion is withdrawn.
- The third column of my first contrast run was `/about-you`, which is not a route (it silently falls back to `/onboard`), so `/employer` was never measured in that pass. It is measured now, along with 26 extra axe screens.

## Still not covered

1. **Screen-reader sessions** — NVDA+Chrome, VoiceOver+Safari, TalkBack. The live-region design (one region, five announcement sources, 15 s throttle) is exactly the kind of thing only a real screen reader confirms.
2. **Disabled-user sessions** — the audience this form exists for.
3. **Firefox / Safari / iOS target-size rounding** — 23.2 px on a 1.45rem root is engine-dependent at fractional device pixel ratios.
4. **`prefers-contrast` / forced-colors** — rules exist (`styles.css:272-274`) but no rendering was measured.
